## 🧭 Acquisition Philosophy: The "Trojan Horse" Audit
We never pitch cold retainers directly. High-end villa operators and top brokers are busy and skeptical. Instead, we use high-value, zero-friction discovery offers:
1. **For Villa & Resort Owners:** *"A Free 15-Minute Direct Booking Leak Audit"* (A short personalized video breakdown showing where they lose direct bookings).
2. **For Licensed Brokers:** *"Free 3-Day Trial Territory Lead Dispatch"* (Send them 3 real pre-qualified tenant leads before asking for a subscription).

## 🎣 Sourcing Strategy: Where to Find High-Value Prospects

### 1. Luxury Villa Operators & Boutique Resorts:
- **Airbnb & Vrbo Scraping:** Search tier-1 luxury hubs (Miami, Madeira, Dubai, Marbella, Cotswolds, Bali) for properties with:
  - Nightly rate > $350/night.
  - Reviews > 30 (high occupancy, established revenue).
  - Lack of a branded direct booking website (they rely 100% on OTA listings).
- **Instagram Geotags & Hospitality Hashtags:** Search `#luxuryvillamiami`, `#madeiravillas`, `#dubaivacationhomes`. Direct outreach to property managers.
- **Google Maps:** Search *"Boutique resort"*, *"Luxury chalets"*, *"Private villa rentals"*. Check their website speed on Google PageSpeed Insights—if slow (>3s), they are hemorrhaging bookings.

### 2. Licensed Real Estate Brokers & Agents:
- **Local MLS & Brokerage Directories:** Century 21, RE/MAX, Keller Williams, Sotheby's International, Engel & Völkers, Christie's.
- **LinkedIn Sales Navigator:** Search *Title: Real Estate Agent / Broker*, *Geography: Miami, London, Lisbon, Dubai, Sydney*.
- **Instagram / TikTok Property Creators:** High-producing agents actively posting property walk-throughs who need steady buyers and tenants.

## ⚡ Closing Funnel & Conversion SOP
1. **Initial Response Received:** Move immediately to WhatsApp audio note or 10-minute screen-share call.
2. **For Audits:** Deliver Loom video within 24 hours. Conclude with a clear CTA to initiate the **$350/mo retainer** or **$199 turnkey launch**.
3. **For Brokers:** Dispatch 2 test leads immediately. Follow up 48 hours later: *"Did you connect with [Lead Name]? Ready to lock in the daily quota subscription before the city slot closes?"*
4. **Onboarding:** Send automated Stripe subscription link + onboarding form to capture domain credentials and WhatsApp numbers.
